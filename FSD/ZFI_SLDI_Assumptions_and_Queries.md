# Subsidiaries Loan and Deemed Investment Schedule - Assumptions and Queries

**Project:** OVL | **Source:** SAP_FIORI_-_Subsidiaries_Loan_and_Deemed_Investment_Schedule_-_Annual_FY26.xlsx
(sheets *Basis of Extraction*, *Loan & Interest Movement*) | **Date:** 20.11.2026 | **Author:** Arnav Johri

Delivery route: a custom data source (cube) plus three queries in the Fiori app *Custom Analytical
Queries*, one per schedule. No development starts until the queries below are answered.

| # | Query | From the workbook | Assumed if no answer | What goes wrong if the assumption is wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | G/L 91905 is shared by Imperial Energy and ONGC Videsh Singapore, 391908 by OVRL and BREML. Which field tells the subsidiaries apart: trading partner, assignment, or only the item text? If text, which words identify each subsidiary? | Accrued Interest block, col. K/L "Bifurcation based on Text" | Item text, matched against words kept in a maintenance table (e.g. `*BREML*`). Lines matching no word show on a row "Unassigned". | A line with a text the table does not know lands on "Unassigned" until the word is added. A separate G/L or a trading partner per subsidiary would remove the dependency on text. |
| 2 | Which words in the item text decide the movement column: Additional Funding, Repayment / Conversion / Interest, Interest recognised, Conversion to Equity Shares, Conversion to Preference Shares, Conversion to Loan? | *Basis of Extraction* row 44; "Bifurcation based on Text" on all three blocks | Debit = Additional Funding / Interest recognised, credit = Repayment / Conversion. Only the three conversions of the Deemed Investment block are decided by words from the maintenance table; a credit matching none shows in a column "Unclassified". | A reversal of a funding entry shows as a repayment instead of reducing the funding. Totals and closing balance stay correct. |
| 3 | Which G/L account(s) hold "Provision Made" (ONGC Narmada, USD -31,403,156.30), and is there one provision account per subsidiary? | Loan and Interest blocks, col. G | Provision accounts are entered in the maintenance table against the subsidiary row; their balance at the To date is "Provision Made". | Column stays empty until the accounts are named. A provision account shared by several subsidiaries needs the text rule of query 1. |
| 4 | Opening balance is taken from FBL3N open items at key date 01.04.2025. Items posted on 01.04.2025 are then in the opening balance and again in the movement (posting date 01.04.2025 to 31.03.2026). | *Basis of Extraction*, screenshots 1 and 3 | Opening = items posted before the From date and still open on it (i.e. as at 31.03.2025). | If the opening must include 1 April postings, opening + movement no longer equals closing whenever something is posted on 1 April. |
| 5 | Balances are open items (FBL3N), not the G/L balance. Confirmed as the basis? | *Basis of Extraction* row 3 | Yes, as in the workbook: open at key date; movement = every item posted in the period. | If a clearing does not net to zero in USD, opening + movement differs from closing by that amount, as it does in the manual sheet today. |
| 6 | Reporting period: only a full financial year, or any From / To date (quarter, half year)? | Title "Annual FY26" | Two prompts, From date and To date. | None; a full year is entered as 01.04.2025 to 31.03.2026. |
| 7 | USD = second local currency (global currency) of company code OVL; "Actual INR" = company code currency of the same lines; "Exchange Rate Impact" = INR amount divided by USD amount of the column. Confirmed? | Deemed Investment block, cols. M to U | Yes. | Wrong currency column or a rate that is not the one expected. |
| 8 | Deemed Investment shows "Closing Balance" twice: before and after "Conversion to Loan". Both wanted? | cols. G and I | Yes, both. | None. |
| 9 | One query per schedule (Loan, Accrued Interest, Deemed Investment), each with a total row, because the three blocks have different columns. Acceptable? | Sheet layout | Yes. | A single screen with three differently headed blocks is not possible in an analytical query. |
| 10 | Company code OVL only, leading ledger? The hidden sheets (OVRL Loan Repayment Schedule, FY21 data, 80999 equity provision) are not part of the report? | Screenshots: Company Code OVL; hidden sheets | OVL lines only as maintained in the table (other company codes can be added there); hidden sheets out of scope. | Missing rows / missing repayment schedule. |
| 11 | Who maintains the mapping (G/L to subsidiary row, text words, provision accounts) when a new loan or subsidiary is added? | - | Corporate Accounts, through a maintenance transaction (SM30), in each system. | A new G/L does not appear in the report until it is entered. |
